Privacy Policy

Moneybook does not collect any of your data.

Where your data is

  • Every transaction, debt and name you record is stored on your own device.
  • The app has no server and no accounts.
  • The developer cannot see, does not receive and does not store any of your data.

iCloud backup — the only time the app uses the internet

  • The app connects to the internet only to back up your book to your own iCloud, and only if you turn on automatic iCloud backup (Settings → Backup & Restore). It is off by default.
  • The copies are kept in your iCloud account, which Apple manages under Apple's policies.
  • The developer still cannot read your data: this is your private iCloud, not a server of the app.
  • While the book is still empty — as on a newly installed phone — the app looks in your iCloud for a copy to restore. This only reads; nothing is sent.
  • You can turn it off at any time; once it is off the app is fully offline again.
  • You can delete every copy in iCloud from inside the app: Settings → Backup & Restore → Restore from iCloud → Delete every copy in iCloud.
  • The wallpaper you choose is not part of a copy.

When data leaves your device

  • When you turn on iCloud backup, as described above.
  • When you choose Back up to a file in Settings → Backup & Restore and pick where to send the CSV or JSON file yourself.
  • If you back up your iPhone (iCloud or a computer), the app's data may be included in that backup. Backups are handled by Apple under Apple's policies, not the app's.
  • The links on the About screen only open your browser when you tap them. The app sends no data with them.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting the app deletes all of the app's data on the device. Copies in iCloud (if you turned the backup on) are not removed with it: delete them in the app first, as above.
  • To erase the book but keep the app: Settings → Reset app → Erase the whole book → Erase permanently.

What the app does not have

  • No advertising.
  • No analytics, no tracking.
  • No third parties receive any data. iCloud copies are in your own account with Apple.
  • No account, no sign-in.

Reminders and messages

  • The evening reminder is off until you turn it on. It is a notification your iPhone schedules for itself; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • A debt reminder is text you read and send yourself, through the app you choose. Moneybook sends nothing for you.
