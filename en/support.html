Questions

Is my data safe?

Your book stays on your iPhone: the app has no server and no account. If you turn on iCloud backup, a copy sits in your own iCloud, kept by Apple; the developer cannot read it. With it off, the app does not connect to the internet. The book on your phone is protected by your iPhone's own passcode. The app has no lock of its own, so please set a passcode.

What happens when I change phones?

On the old phone: Settings → Backup & Restore → Back up to iCloud, and turn automatic backup on. On the new phone, sign in to the same iCloud account and install the app: Today offers the copy it found. Tap Restore and your book comes back whole. Not using iCloud? Choose Back up to a file on the old phone, send the JSON file to the new one, then Restore from a file.

How do I record a debt paid back in parts?

Open the Debts tab, choose the debt, tap Record a payment, type this payment's amount and tap Save. Each payment is listed separately and the amount left goes down on its own.

Why doesn't lending count as spending?

Lend Anna 3 million and you are not 3 million poorer: you swapped cash for money she owes you. Likewise, borrowed money is not income, because you have to give it back. Mixed together, "how much did I spend this month" would be wrong. Money you lend still lowers your cash balance, and shows on its own line.

Which currencies does it support?

Vietnamese dong only. Amounts are written the Vietnamese way, like 1.000.000 ₫, in both languages. The app has no exchange rates and converts nothing.

How do I back up and restore my book?

On Today, tap the gear to open Settings and choose Backup & Restore. There are two ways. iCloud: automatic, to your own iCloud; the app keeps the latest 5 copies. File: Back up to a file makes a JSON file (restores completely) and a CSV file (opens in Excel or Google Sheets), and you choose where to send them. Restore from a file always shows a preview and can be undone.
