Moneybook: Spend, Lend & Owe

Expenses, loans & who owes you

Who owes you? Who do you owe? Note each entry in seconds, and if you forget a day, add it to that day later. No account, no ads, your data stays on your iPhone.

Download on the App Store

Four buttons, no more

Money in, Money out, Lend, Borrow, right on the first screen. Type the amount, tap Save.

A debt book both ways

"Owed to me" and "I owe": who, how much is left, since when. Record part payments, full payment, or write a debt off.

Six numbers

By Day, Week, Month or Year. Tap a number to see the entries that made it.

Lending is not spending

Money you lend does not count as spending, and money you borrow does not count as income. So "how much did I spend this month" is a true number.

Forgot? Add it to the right day

Open the Day report, go back to the day you missed, tap Money in or Money out. The entry lands on the day you are viewing.

A polite debt reminder

A message written for you with the exact amount left. You read it and choose how to send it; the app sends nothing for you.

Same stretch, last period

Under Spent in Week, Month and Year: the first half of this month against the first half of last month, never half a month against a whole one.

Evening reminder

Off until you turn it on, at the time you choose. On days you have written something down, it stays silent.

Many wallets, one total

Cash, bank, e-wallet. The big number is every wallet together; tap it to see each one. Moving money between wallets is neither spending nor income.

Your own categories

Name them the way you live. Reports filter by wallet, by category, or both.

Home Screen widget

What you have lent and what you owe, without opening the app. Tap it to open your debts.

Backup & Restore

Automatic backup to your own iCloud, or a CSV or JSON file. The developer cannot read your data.

Your data is yours

No account, no ads, no tracking. With iCloud backup off, the app works fully offline.

Light, dark and your own wallpaper

Follows your iPhone, or choose in Settings. Set your own wallpaper; amounts always stay readable.
